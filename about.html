Who we are

Built on engineering, not sales

M2E Ltd is an independent engineering company, incorporated in 2015 and based in Tamworth, Staffordshire. We specialise in controls, schematics, panel design and electrical engineering for the pharmaceutical, baggage, automotive and production industries.

We believe in building partnerships, and that the key to our success lies in our ability to satisfy our clients.

Our mission: “To provide clients with innovative solutions and excellent service at a competitive price. To offer a flexible approach, consistent high quality and a professional service to our clients.”
