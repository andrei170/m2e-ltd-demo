Do you design and build control panels in-house?

Yes. We design the control system, produce the schematics, and our own team of experienced panel builders builds and tests the panels.

Which electrical design software do you use?

Schematics are produced in an electrical drawing package to suit the client’s requirements, principally AutoCAD Electrical or EPLAN.

Do you have a functional safety engineer?

Yes, in-house. Our functional safety engineer can design and validate safety systems, from Sistema calculations to performance levels.

What is a Sistema calculation?

Sistema is a free tool from the German IFA used to calculate the Performance Level (PL) a machine safety function achieves under EN ISO 13849-1. We use it to show a safety function meets the level it needs.

Can you work on an existing panel or machine?

Yes. Our design work integrates with existing projects, upgrades and customisations as well as new builds.

Do you produce installation documentation?

Yes: single line diagrams, E&I diagrams, cable schedules and termination schedules, so external installation teams can deliver the design accurately. We can also supervise installation.

Which industries do you work in?

Baggage conveyors, parcel, food and beverage, pharmaceutical, research and development, special purpose machinery, automotive and production movement.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. We complete a range of projects, from large-scale work to small bespoke jobs.
