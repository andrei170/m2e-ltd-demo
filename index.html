Why one team matters

Safety designed in, not checked at the end

The engineer who validates your safety functions sits with the engineers drawing the schematics and the builders wiring the panel. Safety requirements shape the design from the first sheet, rather than arriving as a list of changes after the panel is built.

All questions
Do you design and build control panels in-house?

Yes. We design the control system, produce the schematics, and our own panel builders build and test the panels.

Which drawing software do you use?

Principally AutoCAD Electrical or EPLAN, to suit your requirements and company standards.

Can you do the functional safety as well?

Yes. Our in-house functional safety engineer designs and validates safety systems, from Sistema calculations to performance levels.

A panel, a drawing package or a safety assessment to scope?

Send us the spec, or just the outline. You talk to an engineer, not a sales desk.
